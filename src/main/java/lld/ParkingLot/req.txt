
Parking Lot LLD

Core Requirements -

A parking lot has multiple parking slots and can be of different types as well
- Car Parking Slot, Bike Parking Slot, Taxi Parking Slot, etc.

Parking lot will manage the parking slots

Allows for parking of vehicles of different types - CAR, BIKE, TAXI

Charges the money for the vehicle parked, when the vehicle entered the parking or
maybe when it is exiting the parking

Supports different payment channels

Can have dynamic pricing strategy as well for the duration of the vehicle parked

Core Entities can be -
Parking lot, Parking slots, Vehicle
Different payment modes, dynamic pricing strategy
